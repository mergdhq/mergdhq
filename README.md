# Mergd

**Hire people by what they've actually built.**

Mergd is a hiring platform that matches people on real work, from GitHub and LeetCode to portfolios, instead of keyword resumes. No job posts. No applicant piles.

[Website](https://mergd.in/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile) · [X](https://x.com/getmergd) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/getmergd) · [Email](mailto:mergdhq@gmail.com)

---

### How it works

- **For candidates:** send your CV and links to your work once, on WhatsApp. Mergd reads your actual work and asks you first when a role fits. Nothing is shared until you say yes. Free, always.
- **For startups:** describe the role in plain words, no job post needed. Get a shortlist of people matched on what they've built, filtered on pay, location and notice period, who have already said yes.

### Why Mergd

- **Evidence, not keywords:** every score links to the work behind it.
- **Consent first:** a company only sees you after you say yes.
- **No ghosting:** everyone who says yes gets a clear answer, with feedback if it's a no.
- **No surprises:** pay, location and notice dealbreakers are captured before any match.
